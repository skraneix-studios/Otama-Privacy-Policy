Privacy Policy — Otama

Effective Date: June 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Otama (“the App”) is a focus-oriented home screen launcher and app-blocking tool developed and provided by Skraneix Development Studios (“we”, “us”, “our”). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Otama is a fully local, offline app. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you create — your blocked apps list, routines, widgets, notes, habits, themes, focus history — stays on your device.

2. Information We Collect

We do NOT collect any personal information, including but not limited to:

  • Name, email, or phone number
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers (advertising ID, IMEI, MAC address, etc.)
  • Usage analytics
  • Crash reports
  • Which apps you have installed on your device
  • Which apps you block, time-lock, or focus on
  • Content of notes, to-dos, habits, countdowns, scratch pads, or any widget
  • Wallpapers or theme choices
  • Mini-game scores or focus session history

All content you create stays entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers — we do not run any servers to send to.

3. Permissions and How They Are Used

Otama requests several Android permissions to function as a launcher and focus tool. Each is used strictly on your device, never transmitted to us or any third party:

Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

Required to detect which app you have just opened, so Otama can apply your configured interrupt-lock or time-lock to that app. Usage information is read in real-time and never logged, stored, or transmitted off the device.

Display Over Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Required to draw the interrupt-lock overlay — the mini-games, breathing exercises, confirmation prompts, and timer screens — on top of the app you opened. The overlay is shown only by Otama, only on your device.

Default Home App

Required for Otama to function as your home screen launcher. Setting Otama as your default launcher is optional and can be reverted at any time in Android Settings.

Post Notifications

Used to display Pomodoro timer notifications, focus reminders, time-lock expiry alerts, and similar in-app messages. All notifications are generated on your device only.

Query All Packages

Required to display your installed apps in the app drawer and let you choose which apps to block, time-lock, or add to Quick Nav. The list of installed apps is read locally and never transmitted.

Foreground Service / Boot Completed

Used to keep the app-monitoring service running so that locks continue working, and to restart the service after your device reboots. No data leaves the device.

4. Local Data Storage

All data created within the App is stored locally on your device, in Android’s standard private app storage. This includes:

  • Your list of blocked apps and their interrupt-lock configurations
  • Time-lock schedules and routines
  • Widget content — notes, to-dos, habit logs, countdowns, scratch pads, saved links, and similar
  • Home screen layout, selected wallpaper, and theme preference
  • Focus history and earn-time game progress
  • Quick Nav slot assignments
  • Premium subscription status (cached for offline use)

This data is fully under your control. Uninstalling the App removes all of it from your device. If you have enabled Android’s system-wide app backup, that backup is handled by Google under their own privacy terms — we have no access to it.

5. Premium Subscriptions and Purchases

Otama offers three premium tiers:

  • Monthly subscription — auto-renews monthly until cancelled.
  • Yearly subscription — auto-renews annually until cancelled.
  • Lifetime purchase — one-time payment, no recurring charges.

All purchases are processed securely through the Google Play Store. We do not receive:

  • Your payment method or billing details
  • Your name, email, or Google account information
  • Any personal identity information

The App only knows whether your current device has an active subscription or lifetime purchase, based on what Google Play Billing reports. This status is cached locally to unlock premium features in the App.

You can cancel any subscription at any time through your Google Play account settings. Lifetime purchases cannot be cancelled (they are one-time payments) but are subject to Google Play’s standard refund policies.

6. No Account Required

The App does not require:

  • Account creation
  • Login or sign-up
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Any personal information

The App is fully functional offline. The only network activity is communication with Google Play Billing when verifying subscription status — initiated by Google’s billing library, not by us.

7. No Data Sharing, Selling, or Tracking

We do NOT:

  • Share your data with anyone
  • Sell your data
  • Use analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, none)
  • Use advertising SDKs or display ads
  • Use crash-reporting tools (no Crashlytics, no Sentry, none)
  • Track your behavior or build behavioral profiles
  • Use cookies or device identifiers for tracking purposes

Because we collect no data, there is nothing for us to share or sell.

8. Network Activity

Otama is offline-first. The only network calls the App makes are to Google Play Billing services, to verify subscription and lifetime-purchase status. No other servers, telemetry endpoints, or content APIs are contacted by the App.

9. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal information on our servers, the following applies:

GDPR (EU and UK Users)

Under GDPR / UK-GDPR you have rights such as access, deletion, portability, and objection. However, since we do not collect or store any personal data, there is no data for us to provide, modify, or delete. To remove all locally stored data, uninstall the App.

CCPA / CPRA (California Users)

We do not collect personal information as defined under CCPA / CPRA. We do not sell or share personal information. California users may contact us, but we have no personal data to disclose or delete.

CalOPPA / Do Not Track

We do not track users across sites or services. The App does not respond to Do Not Track (DNT) signals because the App does not track in the first place.

10. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The App is intended for general productivity use and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Third-Party Services

The App uses one third-party service:

  • Google Play Billing — handles all subscription and one-time purchases. Refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles payment data.

No advertising networks, analytics services, crash reporters, content delivery networks, or other third-party SDKs are used.

12. Security

Because no data is transmitted to or stored on our servers, your information remains fully under your control on your device. We recommend securing your device with a passcode, PIN, or biometric lock to protect the content you create within the App.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App’s behavior changes — for example, if we add a new permission or feature in a future version. If changes occur, the updated version will be posted with a new “Effective Date”. Significant changes will be surfaced in the App where appropriate.
